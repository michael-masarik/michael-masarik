The Card Catalog is a simple, [Astro-based](https://astro.build) site hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Design is [shadcn/ui](https://ui.shadcn.com, and podcast players are built with [audio-ui](https://www.audio-ui.dev). Nothing super fancy, but a simple project that I maintain.
